About

About

I'm responsible for the network and systems infrastructure at Allg Connect Telecom, a Brazilian ISP, where I lead a team and oversee everything involved in keeping an ISP operation running: monitoring (Zabbix), customer and OS management (IXC Provider), and the favorite headache of every NOC — link failover.

In recent years I've been pulling this operational experience into cloud and automation: AWS certifications, a DevSecOps capstone through the Hackers do Bem program (SENAI/RNP/Softex), and an ongoing degree in Systems Analysis and Development.

I enjoy sharing this publicly, whether through open-source tools that solve real NOC problems, or technical content for the IT community.
